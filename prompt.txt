Create a platform for the "Ministry of Land Use Planning: Building Permits" (MATAC) website. The landing page is non-scrolling, functioning like a PowerPoint presentation. It features "Log In" and "Sign Up" buttons, as well as icons for dark/light mode and language translation.
It includes the text: "Designed for ministries in charge of urban planning, Malagasy municipalities, regional technical services, representatives, and users," along with a section to display the logos of five partners. The primary color is #269745 with a white background; the dark mode background color is #101A2E.
After logging in, the platform features a simple dashboard with a sidebar (covering alignment, building permits, and land-filling permits) and a navigation bar (displaying the user's name, email, and photo, plus notifications, dark mode, and translation options). Additional pages include tables for adding entries, searching, filtering, etc., for alignment, building permits, and land-filling permits. The platform is in French.
